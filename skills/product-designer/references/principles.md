# Principles

Cite a principle by name only when it explains the call, and pair it with a concrete example.

## UX
- User flows, information architecture, Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, friction and drop-off, onboarding, forms, empty/error/loading states.

## UI
- Visual hierarchy, spacing and alignment, type scale, color and contrast, consistent components.

## Named principles
- Hierarchy, proximity, contrast, alignment, repetition, white space.
- Hick's law (fewer choices, faster decisions), Fitts's law (bigger, closer targets), Jakob's law (people expect what they know from other apps), progressive disclosure, peak-end rule.

## Conversion and trust
- CTAs, social proof, pricing display, reducing perceived effort.
- For Aisle, tie to willingness to pay and the wedge in the glossary.

## Honest AI design
- Aisle estimates bouquets from photos. Show an estimate as an estimate: ranges, confidence, what it is based on.
- Let users correct the AI. Explain failures in plain words. Avoid false precision and dark patterns.
