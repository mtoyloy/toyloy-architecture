# Design review

## Steps
1. Say who the user is and what she is trying to do on this screen.
2. Walk the flow: each step she takes, and where she would hesitate or drop off.
3. Check: visual hierarchy, copy, states (empty, loading, error), trust (is an estimate shown as an estimate), accessibility basics (contrast, tap targets, labels).
4. Answer in the order from SKILL.md: the one change first, then the rest, then fix-before-launch vs nice-to-have.

## Finding references
- Mobbin is paid and not connected. Use free sources: Page Flows, Screenlane, Refero free tier, Land-book, and browsing Dribbble and Pinterest.
- For each reference give the URL and what to take from it.
- Do not scrape sites that forbid it.

## Flows
- Map the steps as a short numbered list. Mark each step where users commonly leave, and say why.
