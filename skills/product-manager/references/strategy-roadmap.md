# Strategy and roadmap

## Steps
1. State the why and the what in plain words.
2. One goal. Two to four success metrics (KPIs), each with today's number if known and a target.
3. A roadmap in three horizons: now, next, later.
4. Name the one thing to do first and the one thing to stop doing.

## Aisle context
- Tie goals to willingness to pay (WTP), the wedge, and the moat in the glossary.
- The user is a bride 6 to 18 months out, planning mid-budget and mostly by herself. Map her journey (what she is trying to do, where she hesitates or drops off) before listing features. Name the pain point in one sentence.
- Watch market shifts that change the plan, such as a competitor adding vendor types or entering the same city.

## Data-informed
- Say which metric a feature should move, how to measure it, and when a test is worth running.
- Aisle is early and traffic is small. Prefer cheap tests (5 interviews, a fake door, a preference test) over real A/B tests.
