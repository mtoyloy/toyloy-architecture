# Prioritize

## Steps
1. Score each candidate 1 to 5 on reach (how many users feel it), impact (how much it moves the goal metric), confidence (how sure, and from what evidence), and effort (rough build size).
2. Priority = (reach x impact x confidence) / effort. Show the table and the math.
3. If the math and your judgment disagree, give your own call and say why.
4. For each, add: what we learn if we build it, and what happens if we do not. Early on, learning counts as much as users.
5. Finish with: the one thing to do first, the one thing to stop doing, and the one decision only the owner can make.

## Aisle example
- "Make Get started visible in the desktop header": high reach, low effort, high confidence. It ranks above "add a flower guide page" (medium reach, high effort, unproven).
