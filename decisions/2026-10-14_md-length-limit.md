# Instruction files stay under 200 lines, 150 preferred. Longer ones get split.

- Date: 2026-10-14
- Decided by: Shelly
- Why: Short files load fast and stay current.
- Replaces: No limit. Earlier trims were done ad hoc.
- How a long file gets split:
  - A repeatable procedure becomes its own skill.
  - A role that needs its own tools or model becomes its own agent.
  - Reference material goes in a separate file, linked by plain path and read on demand.
